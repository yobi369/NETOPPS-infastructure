# NETOPPS Institutional Licensing Options

This document explains the licensing paths at a commercial discussion level. It is not a licence grant, binding offer, legal opinion, or price list.

## Option 1 — Community edition (AGPL-3.0-or-later)

Suitable for organizations comfortable with the AGPL terms and their obligations.

- Use, inspect, modify, and redistribute the covered community software in accordance with AGPL-3.0-or-later.
- Commercial use is permitted when licence conditions are met; AGPL is not a non-commercial-use restriction.
- Section 13 includes source-code obligations for modified versions used by people interacting with them remotely over a network.
- Third-party components remain under their respective licences.
- No support, implementation, maintenance, warranty, or SLA is included merely by using the community edition.

Customers should obtain their own legal review of how AGPL applies to their deployment and distribution model.

## Option 2 — Separately negotiated commercial licence

An organization may request a separate written agreement where it requires terms different from the community licence. The final agreement can define a specific combination of:

- authorized legal entities and affiliates;
- sites, installations, environments, and users;
- on-premises or private-environment deployment;
- internal modification and source-code access;
- embedding, redistribution, or service-provider rights;
- release and update access;
- implementation, training, maintenance, and support services;
- term, renewal, fees, and payment schedule.

Only rights expressly written in a signed agreement are included. A commercial licence does not automatically include hosting, integrations, source escrow, support, custom development, or an SLA.

## Possible commercial packaging structure

These are packaging concepts to scope a customer conversation, not existing SKUs or priced products.

| Package concept | Intended buyer | Possible scope to negotiate |
|---|---|---|
| Institutional Assessment | One agency, campus, or enterprise team | Defined deployment scope, agreed release, one baseline assessment workflow, report handover, and optional analyst orientation |
| Multi-Site Resilience | Organization managing several sites or service groups | Multi-site modelling scope, baseline/candidate comparisons, agreed number of service models, and workshop |
| Enterprise / Operator | Large enterprise, telecom, or service provider | Organization-wide or defined business-unit rights, larger model scope, deployment support, integration discovery, and negotiated maintenance |
| Strategic / OEM | Vendor or integrator embedding or redistributing NETOPPS | Explicit embedding/redistribution rights, product boundaries, sublicensing conditions, support responsibilities, and commercial terms |

Package names and scopes must be finalized per customer. They do not imply that multi-tenant hosting, live telemetry, carrier integrations, or OEM capabilities are currently implemented.

## Commercial pricing model to evaluate

The project has not published prices. For a customer-specific quote, evaluate:

1. Licence metric: organization, site, installation, operator, or defined deployment.
2. Licensed components and release.
3. Number of sites, service models, environments, and authorized users.
4. Whether source access and internal modifications are required.
5. Whether implementation, data preparation, training, or workshops are requested.
6. Support coverage, hours, response targets, maintenance, and update entitlement.
7. Contract duration, renewal, taxes, and procurement requirements.

Avoid quoting until the rights holder has confirmed ownership and counsel has reviewed the relevant agreement.

## Procurement and assurance information

Before contracting, prepare as applicable:

- legal identity and authority of the licensor;
- ownership and contributor-rights evidence;
- third-party dependency licence inventory and release-specific SBOM;
- version, release identifier, artifact checksums, and supported runtime;
- deployment, backup, update, and security documentation;
- data flow and data classification statement;
- customer-specific acceptance criteria;
- support scope and escalation process, if purchased;
- signed commercial agreement and any data/security addenda.

## Important exclusions unless separately implemented and contracted

- hosted SaaS or multi-tenant access;
- central identity, SSO, or role-based organization accounts;
- live monitoring, telemetry collection, or network discovery;
- device configuration, routing control, or automated failover;
- carrier, cloud, or vendor integrations;
- certified security, resilience, or regulatory compliance;
- guaranteed uptime, response, restoration, or business outcomes.
