Guided product demonstration · synthetic environment

What happens when a critical site fails?

Choose a disruption scenario, compare a baseline design with a proposed alternate path, and inspect how modeled services and capacity respond. This guided experience explains the resilience questions NETOPPS is designed to help teams investigate.

Demonstration boundary: This is a browser-based illustrative model using synthetic services, assets and capacities. It is not connected to the NETOPPS analysis engine, a live network, or any operator environment. Results are deterministic examples, not measured availability, outage forecasts or failover guarantees.

1 · Select a disruption

All modeled assets assigned to the synthetic east site are unavailable. Shared site dependencies are treated as a single failure domain.

2 · Compare a design

Baseline: each service has one modeled dependency path through the east site. No alternate path is declared in this example.

What must be verified in a real evaluation?

Physical route and power independence, alternate-path reachability, actual busy-hour capacity, service dependencies and approved recovery objectives must be validated by the operator.
