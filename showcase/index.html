The product

Infrastructure decisions need more than a topology diagram.

A network map shows connections. NETOPPS helps teams reason about what those connections support, what a selected failure could affect, and which recovery dependencies matter most.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Map critical dependencies

Represent network components, sites, services and their relationships so analysis reflects institutional service importance, not just individual links.

02 / TEST

Explore disruption scenarios

Model selected node, link and site failures; trace affected services, dependency paths and potential cascading impacts.

03 / PRIORITISE

Plan resilience improvements

Assess declared redundancy, failover capacity, failure domains and operator-defined recovery priorities to inform planning discussions.

Analysis capabilities

Turn infrastructure models into actionable questions.

NETOPPS structures technical analysis into outputs that engineering and leadership teams can review together.

1

Structural risk analysis

Compare topology versions and identify isolated elements, weak points and structural changes.

2

Service impact analysis

Trace dependencies and estimate which modeled institutional services are affected by selected failures.

3

Redundancy and capacity review

Examine declared alternate paths, failover states and capacity remaining under modeled scenarios.

4

Recovery prioritisation

Use service criticality and operator-defined RTO/RPO objectives to organise recovery planning.

Work with NETOPPS

Start with a focused institutional evaluation.

A pilot begins with a defined question and a representative, non-sensitive model. The evaluation can then document assumptions, scenario results, limitations and potential resilience improvements.

Current deployment model: NETOPPS is a locally run, operator-driven Python analysis toolkit that produces offline results and executive briefing material. It is not currently a hosted SaaS dashboard, live network monitoring service, network controller or automated failover platform.
01

Scope the evaluation

Agree on a representative model, priority services and specific failure scenarios.

02

Run structured analysis

Execute the local toolkit against supplied topology and service data.

03

Review findings

Validate assumptions with institutional engineers and agree on evidence-led next steps.

Analysis depends on operator-supplied topology, traffic, capacity, criticality and recovery data. Outputs are planning evidence, not outage probabilities, certification or guarantees of restoration performance.
