Network infrastructure · Resilience engineering

Understand what fails.
Plan what recovers.

NETOPPS is an infrastructure analysis toolkit for teams that need to inspect network designs, model disruption scenarios, understand critical service dependencies and prioritise recovery options — before an incident.

Explore the GitHub repository ↗ Discuss an institutional evaluation
Current product scope: NETOPPS is a local, operator-driven Python CLI and offline analysis toolkit. It is not a hosted monitoring service, network controller or automated failover system. Results depend on the quality of supplied topology and service data.
What it helps teams examine

From topology visibility to recovery priorities.

Use structured, reproducible analysis to explore how infrastructure design choices may affect important services during disruption.

01 / DESIGN

Topology and structural risk

Compare network designs, inspect connectivity paths, identify isolated components and highlight structural fragility in supplied models.

02 / DISRUPTION

Failure scenario analysis

Model selected node, link and site failures, trace affected dependencies and examine potential cascading service impacts.

03 / RESILIENCE

Recovery and capacity planning

Evaluate declared redundancy, capacity shortfalls, service criticality and operator-defined recovery objectives.

Potential institutional applications

Designed for serious infrastructure conversations.

NETOPPS can support structured technical evaluations by teams responsible for important digital and network services.

Government ICTUniversitiesTelecommunicationsEnterprise ITInfrastructure teams

Questions an evaluation can explore

  • Which modeled services depend on a single component or failure domain?
  • Where could additional redundancy improve modeled resilience?
  • What declared capacity is available after selected failures?
  • Which services should receive recovery attention first?
Evaluation pathway

Start with a bounded, evidence-led pilot.

STEP 1

Define the scope

Agree on a non-sensitive, representative topology and the services or scenarios to examine.

STEP 2

Run the analysis

Use the local toolkit to produce reproducible scenario results and an offline executive briefing.

STEP 3

Review the evidence

Validate assumptions with the institution's engineers and document limitations, findings and next steps.
