## Operational use

- Obtain a completed agreement before merging an external contribution intended to be eligible for commercial relicensing.
- Keep the signed agreement and the exact contribution / pull request reference in a restricted-access project records location.
- Do not publish identity documents, private addresses, signatures, or other personal information in the public repository.
- If a contributor declines, their contribution may be accepted only under a separately approved path that does not assume commercial relicensing rights; otherwise do not merge it into the dual-licensed codebase.
- Have qualified counsel review this template, including local law, electronic signatures, patent language, and the project's actual ownership chain, before using it.
