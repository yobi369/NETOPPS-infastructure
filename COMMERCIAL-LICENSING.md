# Commercial licensing

NETOPPS Infrastructure is offered under a dual-licensing model. This page explains the available paths; it is not itself a commercial licence or an offer with fixed pricing.

## Community edition

The current community edition is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0-or-later). See [LICENSE-AGPL-3.0.txt](LICENSE-AGPL-3.0.txt).

AGPL permits commercial use, modification, and redistribution when its conditions are followed. It is not a non-commercial-use restriction. In particular, section 13 addresses source-code offers for modified versions used by people interacting with them remotely over a network. Customers may choose this community licence without purchasing a separate commercial licence.

## Separate commercial licence

An organization that requires terms different from AGPL may request a separate written commercial agreement from the copyright holder. Depending on the negotiated scope, it may cover proprietary deployment, internal modification, embedding, redistribution, affiliate use, implementation services, support, maintenance, or other expressly stated rights.

A commercial licence is effective only when a written agreement is completed and signed by authorized representatives. No pricing, service-level commitment, warranty, indemnity, exclusivity, support response target, or other commercial term is promised by this repository. Blank or unsigned templates grant no rights.

See the [Commercial Licence Agreement template](docs/COMMERCIAL-LICENSE-AGREEMENT-TEMPLATE.md) for the proposed structure and the [Commercial Readiness Checklist](docs/COMMERCIAL-READINESS-CHECKLIST.md) for items that must be completed before customer contracting. The template is a discussion aid, not legal advice; it must be reviewed and completed by qualified counsel.

## Enquiries

Contact the copyright holder through the GitHub profile linked from this repository. An enquiry should identify:

- the organization's full legal name and country;
- intended deployment model and environment;
- the exact components and version required;
- whether source access, internal modifications, embedding, affiliate access, or redistribution are required;
- expected number of sites, installations, and operators;
- desired support, maintenance, training, or implementation scope.

Do not send credentials, production topology, personal data, or confidential network diagrams in an initial enquiry.

## Scope and contributor rights

A commercial licence can cover only material for which the licensor has sufficient rights. Third-party dependencies and assets remain governed by their own licences. The project uses a Contributor License Agreement for eligible external contributions: contributors retain copyright but grant the project rights holder the permissions described in [CONTRIBUTOR-LICENSE-AGREEMENT.md](CONTRIBUTOR-LICENSE-AGREEMENT.md). The CLA must be completed and accepted before the contribution is merged into the dual-licensed codebase. Contributions without those rights must not be represented as commercially relicensable.

The CLA is a draft template pending qualified legal review. The project must also verify the rights holder's identity and ownership chain for existing material before making customer-specific representations.
